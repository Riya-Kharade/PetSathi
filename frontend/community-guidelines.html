Community Guidelines

Together, let's build a kind, respectful and safe community where every pet and pet lover feels welcome. Your kindness makes PetSathi a better place for everyone.

Welcome to PetSathi!

PetSathi brings pet owners and walkers together through a shared love for animals. These guidelines explain the behaviour and responsibilities expected from everyone using our platform. Let's create a positive, trustworthy and caring community for all.

1. Respectful Behaviour

Treat every community member with kindness and respect. Communicate politely, respect different opinions, avoid personal attacks and maintain a friendly environment. Everyone deserves to feel welcome.

2. Pet Welfare

Always put pets' health, comfort and safety first. Provide proper food, water, exercise and care. Treat every animal with compassion and never engage in cruelty, neglect or harmful behaviour.

3. Responsible Information Sharing

Share accurate, useful and relevant information. Avoid spreading rumours, false claims or unverified pet-care advice. For medical concerns, always encourage consultation with a qualified veterinarian.

4. Prohibited Activities

Harassment, abusive language, discrimination, spam, fraudulent activities and misleading content are not acceptable. Do not threaten, intimidate or deliberately disturb other community members.

5. Safety Guidelines

Pet owners should share important information about their pet's health, behaviour and walking needs. Walkers should follow agreed instructions, use appropriate safety equipment and handle pets carefully. Always communicate clearly before a walk.

6. Reporting Issues

If you experience inappropriate behaviour or notice a safety concern, contact the PetSathi team through the available contact channels or GitHub repository. Provide clear information about your concern.

7. Content Responsibility

Only share content that you own or have permission to use. Respect other people's privacy and intellectual property. Avoid posting offensive, harmful, misleading or inappropriate material.

8. Consequences of Violations

Violations of these guidelines may lead to appropriate action depending on their seriousness and applicable platform policies. Possible actions may include warnings, restrictions or suspension of access to PetSathi where such measures are available.

9. Contact Information

Have questions or concerns about our Community Guidelines? You can reach the PetSathi team through our project communication channels.

PetSathi GitHub Repository

You can also visit our Contact Us page.

Thank you for helping us build a loving community for pets and their people!
