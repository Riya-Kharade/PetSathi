Accessibility Commitment

Digital Inclusion & User Commitment

At PetSathi, our founding mission is to ensure that compassionate pet care, verified health guides, and trustworthy caregiver networks are reachable for everyone—regardless of physical, sensory, or cognitive ability.

Universal Access by Design

Every page is designed to accommodate users living with permanent, temporary, or situational limitations, ensuring dignity-affirming interaction across all species portals.

W3C Global Standards

We benchmark PetSathi against international Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA) and semantic HTML best practices to ensure broad interoperability.

Barrier-Free Pet Care

Compassionate care guides, vaccination schedules, and dog walker profiles are formatted so no pet owner or companion is ever excluded by sensory gatekeeping.

POUR Framework

Accessibility Approach & POUR Principles

Our engineering and design workflows incorporate accessibility from the initial wireframe stage, anchored in the four foundational W3C pillars of digital inclusion.

P
Pillar 01

Perceivable

All informational content and interface controls must be presented in formats that can be absorbed through multiple senses. We pair high-contrast typography (6.1:1+ ratio) with clear structural hierarchy and descriptive non-text alternatives.

O
Pillar 02

Operable

The interface cannot demand interactions that a user cannot perform. Navigation menus, search filters, and form inputs are 100% operable via a standard keyboard alone, with zero mouse dependency or timed traps.

U
Pillar 03

Understandable

Information and platform operations must be predictable, clear, and logical. Content uses straightforward pet care terminology, consistent navigation menus across all portals, and self-explanatory form feedback.

R
Pillar 04

Robust

Platform code is built with clean HTML5 semantics and valid CSS so it can be reliably interpreted by modern browsers, screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver), screen magnifiers, and emerging assistive tech.

Visual Comfort

Visual Accessibility & Contrast Standards

Ensuring users with low vision, color vision deficiencies, or ambient glare can comfortably perceive every piece of guidance with crystal clarity and zero eye fatigue.

High Contrast Ratios (6.1:1+)

Deep forest green (#286849) and dark charcoal ink (#192b21) paired against soft cream (#fafcf7) surpass WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimums, reaching up to 11.4:1 contrast.

Dual-Font Typographic Hierarchy

Classic Georgia serif headings provide instant visual orientation, paired with clean geometric DM Sans body text for effortless readability across desktop and mobile.

Fluid Zoom up to 200%

Scalable rem units and CSS clamp() functions allow browser zoom magnification up to 200% without horizontal text overflow or visual clipping.

Multi-Modal Signals

Color is never the sole communicator. Interactive links, validation alerts, and status tags always pair color cues with distinctive icons and explicit text labels.

Generous Line Spacing & Sizing

Paragraphs maintain generous line heights of 1.65 to 1.8 with base font sizes of 16px, preventing visual crowding and aiding readers with dyslexia or visual fatigue.

Seizure & Motion Safety

PetSathi contains zero rapidly flashing animations, strobing graphics, or autoplay video, eliminating risks related to photosensitive epileptic seizures (WCAG 2.3.1).

Motor Accessibility

Keyboard Navigation & Focus Flow

Every functional area—from searching pet directories to booking walkers—is 100% operable using only a standard keyboard, ensuring independence for users who do not use a mouse.

Key / Combination Element Targeted Expected Behavior
Tab Links, buttons, form fields Advances focus forward along the natural reading and DOM hierarchy.
Shift + Tab Interactive elements Moves focus backward to the previously focused control.
Enter Links, buttons, form submission Activates the focused link, expands accordions, or submits the form.
Spacebar Buttons, toggles, checkboxes Toggles button states (e.g. mobile drawer menu, FAQ panels).
Escape Open menus & popups Closes mobile navigation drawers and dismisses active flyouts.

Skip to Main Content

A hidden link at the very top of the page becomes visible upon first Tab, allowing keyboard users to bypass repetitive header links.

High-Visibility Focus Rings

Focused interactive elements display a distinct 3px colored ring with comfortable offset, ensuring users always know their exact position on screen.

Zero Keyboard Traps

Users can freely navigate into and out of all forms, search inputs, and dropdown panels without ever getting trapped or requiring a pointer device.

Assistive Compatibility

Screen Reader Support & Semantic HTML

Architected for seamless compatibility with NVDA, JAWS, Apple VoiceOver, and Google TalkBack by prioritizing clean, native HTML5 semantics over complex ARIA hacks.

HTML5 Semantic Landmarks

Pages are structured using standardized <header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, and <footer> tags for rapid landmark hopping.

Strict Heading Progression

Each page maintains a single <h1> followed by logically ordered <h2> and <h3> subheadings without skipped levels, creating a dependable document outline.

Descriptive Alt Text

Informational imagery includes descriptive alt attributes explaining the subject matter, while purely ornamental graphics use aria-hidden="true".

Clear ARIA State Management

Dynamic controls update aria-expanded="true/false" and aria-haspopup="true" so users immediately perceive when navigation menus expand or collapse.

Programmatic Form Labeling

Every input field, selector, and textarea is programmatically linked to a visible <label for="..."> element with helpful placeholder context.

Explicit Action Labels

Icon-only buttons (such as the mobile menu toggle) feature descriptive aria-label tags (e.g. aria-label="Toggle navigation") for clear auditory output.

Viewport Adaptability

Responsive Design & Device Usability

Adaptive fluid layouts engineered to deliver an uncompromising, equivalent experience across 4K monitors, laptops, tablets, and handheld mobile devices.

Viewport Reflow (WCAG 1.4.10)

Content reorganizes seamlessly from multi-column desktop grids into clean single-column mobile streams down to 320px width with zero horizontal scrolling.

Generous 44×44px Touch Targets

Interactive elements—including navigation links, filter pills, and action buttons—meet the 44×44px minimum touch target standard for coarse motor pointers.

Dual Orientation Freedom

The entire platform functions smoothly in both portrait and landscape screen modes without forcing device rotation or restricting viewport flexibility.

Lightweight & Fast Loading

Optimized CSS assets and minimal external dependencies ensure fast page rendering for users accessing pet care on low-bandwidth or metered mobile connections.

Honest Transparency

Known Limitations & Active Remediations

In accordance with our commitment to transparency, we openly document verified technical limitations across PetSathi that our engineering team is actively addressing.

Active Remediation

Dynamic Search Live Alerts

On search-results.html and faq.html, live query filtering updates DOM results instantly without polite aria-live announcements stating exact matching counts.

Active Remediation

Walker Booking Grid Navigation

In booking flows (walker/index.html and walker/handover.html), time slots use button rows rather than full 2D ARIA grid keyboard arrow navigation (ArrowUp/Down/Left/Right).

Layout Redesign

Dense Tables on Narrow Screens

In select guides (such as dog nutrition portions and reptile thermal ranges), multi-column tables require horizontal swipe scrolling on viewports under 360px width.

External Host

Third-Party External Links

External destinations (including GitHub issues, LinkedIn profiles, and third-party walker route maps) operate on host standards outside PetSathi's direct engineering control.

Color Tuning

Pastel Badge Small Text

A select few decorative tags with pastel backgrounds (#edf6df) meet WCAG AA (4.5:1+) for large text, and are being darkened to meet the strict AAA standard (7:1) for small body text.

Form Validation

User-Submitted Media Alt-Text

On community review pages (reviews.html), future community photo submissions may lack author-written alt text. Automated alt-text prompting workflows are in active design.

Community Input

Feedback & Barrier Reporting

We welcome constructive feedback, bug reports, and suggestions from individuals who rely on assistive technologies. Your lived experience helps us prioritize critical fixes.

Helpful Details to Include

  • Page URL or Title: Where the accessibility obstacle was encountered.
  • Task Description: What you were attempting to accomplish (e.g. search walkers).
  • Specific Barrier: The unexpected behavior or roadblock observed.
  • Assistive Tech Details: Your device, browser, and software (e.g. NVDA, VoiceOver).

Our Response Commitment

  • 2-3 Day Response: Our support team acknowledges all accessibility inquiries within 2 to 3 business days.
  • Alternative Formats: Immediate provision of accessible guidance, telephone support, or alternative text formats.
  • Engineering Sprint: Direct escalation of identified roadblocks into our active bug fixing queue.
  • Follow-up Confirmation: We notify you directly once the remediation has been deployed to production.
