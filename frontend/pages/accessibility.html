1. Introduction

PetSathi's accessibility commitment

PetSathi was founded with a community-first spirit: to make reliable pet wellness, breed education, adoption guidance, and walking services readily available to all pet lovers. We consider digital accessibility a core element of user experience, not an optional feature.

We are committed to providing a website that is welcoming and usable by everyone, including people who rely on screen readers, screen magnification, voice recognition software, keyboard-only navigation, switch devices, or specific color contrast preferences.

Core Commitment

Whether you are researching puppy nutrition, discovering gentle cat breeds, scheduling a dog walk, or reviewing care guidelines for exotic pets, we strive to make every interaction seamless, equitable, and dignified.

2. Accessibility Approach

Guiding principles and standards

To ensure consistent accessibility across our web ecosystem, PetSathi uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standard as our principal benchmark. These guidelines are organized under four foundational principles (POUR):

Perceivable

Information and user interface components must be presentable to users in ways they can perceive, through both visual styling and non-visual screen reader text.

Operable

Interface components and navigation must be fully operable using keyboard alone, without reliance on a pointer or touch gestures, and free from keyboard traps.

Understandable

Information and the operation of the user interface must be intuitive, predictable, and written in clear, unambiguous language.

Robust

Content must be robust enough that it can be interpreted reliably by a wide variety of user agents, including assistive technologies, desktop browsers, and mobile devices.

Our development lifecycle incorporates semantic HTML validation, clean CSS architecture, modular components, and accessibility testing during code reviews and community contributions.

3. Visual Accessibility

Contrast, typography, and clear visual hierarchy

PetSathi features a nature-inspired green, cream, and white palette carefully curated to promote calm, readable interfaces that minimize visual fatigue.

  • Color Contrast Ratios: Primary text and interactive components maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against their backgrounds (exceeding 7:1 for headers and deep forest green accents), aligning with WCAG 2.1 AA specifications.
  • Readable Typography: Body copy is rendered in modern sans-serif typefaces (DM Sans and system fallbacks) with generous line heights (1.6 to 1.75) and balanced letter spacing to aid individuals with dyslexia or low vision.
  • Non-Reliance on Color Alone: Color is never the sole medium used to convey crucial information, error states, or interactive cues. Alerts and system statuses pair distinct colors with descriptive icons and text labels.
  • Zoom & Magnification: The layout is designed to scale gracefully when users zoom up to 200% in their browser settings without truncation or broken horizontal scrolling.

4. Keyboard Navigation

Full keyboard access without requiring a mouse

All key interactions and content areas on PetSathi are engineered to be reachable and operable using keyboard input alone. Users can navigate without needing a mouse, trackpad, or touch screen.

Logical Tab Order

Interactive elements follow a natural top-to-bottom, left-to-right reading order matching visual presentation.

Visible Focus Indicators

Active elements display clear focus rings, ensuring keyboard navigators always know their location.

Skip to Content

A "Skip to main content" bypass link is available as the first tab stop, allowing users to jump directly to page content.

Accessible Menus

Navigation dropdowns and mobile toggles update their aria-expanded states and can be triggered via keyboard.

5. Screen Reader Support

Semantic markup, ARIA landmarks, and assistive compatibility

We build with semantic HTML5 elements to ensure screen reader software—such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack—can accurately convey page hierarchy and meaning:

  • Landmark Structure: Proper use of <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, and <footer> allows assistive users to navigate by landmarks efficiently.
  • Heading Hierarchy: Pages feature a singular <h1> representing the primary page subject, followed by structured <h2> and <h3> subtitles without skipped levels.
  • Image Alternative Text: Meaningful images include descriptive alt text detailing their subject matter. Decorative elements, icons, and SVGs are marked with aria-hidden="true" to prevent auditory clutter.
  • Form Accessibility: Contact inputs, login fields, and search boxes feature explicit labels, descriptive placeholder hints, and clear error associations.

6. Responsive Design

Consistent accessibility across all screen sizes and orientations

PetSathi adapts smoothly across diverse devices, screen sizes, and orientations:

  • Flexible Grid Systems: Layouts adapt dynamically from 4K desktop displays and laptops down to tablets and mobile phones without horizontal overflow.
  • Accessible Touch Targets: Interactive elements (buttons, links, form controls) are sized with adequate padding (minimum 44×44 CSS pixels) to facilitate accurate touch interaction for individuals with motor impairments.
  • Responsive Navigation Drawer: On small screens, navigation folds into an accessible slide-out drawer with a high-contrast toggle button and keyboard trapping considerations.
  • Device Orientation: The site operates seamlessly in both portrait and landscape modes on mobile and tablet devices.

7. Known Limitations

Transparent assessment of ongoing challenges

While we strive for comprehensive accessibility, PetSathi is an actively evolving open-source community platform. In the spirit of transparency, we acknowledge the following known limitations currently undergoing remediation:

Active Remediation Areas

We are actively working to address these items in upcoming release sprints. Community contributions and feedback on these areas are warmly welcomed.

  • Third-Party Components: External links, mapping widgets, and CDN-hosted assets may occasionally have accessibility constraints outside our direct codebase control.
  • Legacy Media Alt Attributes: A subset of community-contributed pet breed photographs may feature brief or generic alternative descriptions. We are currently performing a comprehensive media audit.
  • Dark Mode & High-Contrast Toggle: While our green and cream palette meets contrast thresholds, a dedicated high-contrast or system dark-mode theme switcher is currently in active development.
  • Dynamic Filtering Live Regions: Certain dynamic search filters (e.g. breed search or pet-walker filters) are being updated with expanded aria-live polite regions to announce real-time result counts to screen readers.

8. Feedback & Suggestions

How to report barriers and suggest enhancements

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of PetSathi. If you encounter any accessibility barrier, difficulty accessing specific content, or have suggestions for improvements, please reach out to us:

  • What to include: Please provide the webpage URL, the device and operating system you are using, the assistive technology used (if applicable), and a brief description of the issue encountered.
  • Response commitment: We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 48 hours and work collaboratively with contributors to implement fixes promptly.

9. Future Improvements

Our ongoing accessibility roadmap

Continuous improvement is a pillar of PetSathi's development ethos. Our upcoming accessibility roadmap includes:

CI/CD Automated Audits

Integrating Axe-Core and Google Lighthouse accessibility checks into our GitHub Actions deployment pipeline to catch regressions early.

Sitewide Theme Switcher

Introducing a native high-contrast and dark theme option respecting prefers-color-scheme user preferences.

Enhanced Skip Links

Refining sitewide skip-navigation targets across all inner subpages and multi-step forms.

Contributor Guidelines

Adding dedicated accessibility best practices to CONTRIBUTING.md to guide future student and community contributors.
