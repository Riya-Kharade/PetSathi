Standards & Expectations

Please review these ten essential guidelines to ensure our platform remains enjoyable, welcoming, and secure for everyone.

Section 01

Welcome to PetSathi

PetSathi is a community-driven, open-source platform created to bring pet parents and trusted companions together. Whether you are finding a walker, learning species care, or sharing companionship notes, you are part of a shared mission to improve animals' daily lives.

  • We celebrate diversity, compassion, and animal welfare across all pet species.
  • Every member contributes to a positive, reliable, and welcoming environment.
  • We believe responsible companionship makes pet care accessible for everyone.
Section 02

Respectful Behaviour

Healthy communities begin with kind words and considerate actions. Treat fellow pet lovers, service providers, and administrators with the same respect and patience you would expect in return.

  • Communicate politely and constructively in all messages and reviews.
  • Respect each person's boundaries, schedules, and caregiving preferences.
  • Address differences professionally and with empathy.
Section 03

Pet Welfare First

The health, emotional comfort, and physical safety of animals under our care are non-negotiable priorities. We advocate exclusively for humane, positive-reinforcement handling.

  • Ensure pets have timely access to clean hydration, shade, and nutrition.
  • Strictly avoid harsh corrections, physical punishment, or neglectful practices.
  • Observe weather safety — avoid hot pavement and excessive humidity during walks.
Section 04

Responsible Information Sharing

Transparent information prevents misunderstandings and protects animals. Owners and caregivers are expected to provide truthful, up-to-date descriptions of pets and capabilities.

  • Disclose behavioural traits, triggers, anxieties, and medical histories honestly.
  • Share accurate walker credentials, experience, and service availability.
  • Cite reliable veterinary advice rather than circulating unverified pet care myths.
Section 05

Prohibited Activities

To preserve user safety and trust, the following actions are strictly prohibited on PetSathi and will trigger swift remedial action:

  • Harassment & Abusive Language: Threatening, using abusive language, discriminating against, or bullying any member.
  • Spam & Solicitations: Unsolicited advertisements, fraudulent services, or automated bot messages.
  • Animal Mistreatment: Any form of cruelty, endangerment, or intentional neglect.
  • Misleading Identity: Impersonating others or falsifying background and pet details.
Section 06

Safety Guidelines

Safe interactions between owners and companions build confident relationships. Take prudent steps before, during, and after every engagement.

  • Pre-Walk Meet & Greet: Introduce pets to their caregiver in a neutral, calm setting first.
  • Equipment Checks: Use well-fitting harnesses, secure leashes, and ID tags at all times.
  • Emergency Readiness: Keep emergency veterinary contact numbers easily accessible.
Section 07

Reporting Issues

If you encounter inappropriate communication, deceptive listings, or pet safety concerns, please notify our team promptly. We take every inquiry seriously.

  • Submit an inquiry via our official Contact Page.
  • Include clear dates, usernames, and a brief description of what took place.
  • For life-threatening pet emergencies, immediately contact local animal services or a licensed veterinarian.
Need to report an issue or concern? Contact Support Team
Section 08

Content Responsibility

Users are responsible for all content, images, messages, and reviews they publish on PetSathi. Keep shared media truthful, safe, and family-friendly.

  • Post authentic photos of your pets, walks, or caregiver experiences.
  • Do not post copyrighted images or content without proper authorization.
  • Respect private property and personal privacy in photos and walking logs.
Section 09

Consequences of Violations

To ensure PetSathi remains a dependable refuge for pets and people, violations of these guidelines result in fair, graded actions based on severity:

  • First Warning: Guidance notice explaining the infraction and required correction.
  • Content Removal: Inappropriate or non-compliant posts or profiles taken down.
  • Account Suspension: Temporary suspension from booking or offering services.
  • Permanent Termination: Complete account ban and reporting to relevant authorities in cases of animal harm.
Section 10

Contact Information

Have questions about these guidelines, suggestions for our community standards, or require help? Our dedicated team is happy to assist.
