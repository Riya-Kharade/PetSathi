Pet Care & Health

Reliable guidance on nutrition, veterinary care, preventative wellness, and daily husbandry.

We have dedicated nutrition manuals for each animal category under our Explore Pets hub. For instance:

  • Dogs: Protein balance, portion sizing, and toxic foods to avoid (such as chocolate, xylitol, and onions).
  • Cats: Moisture-rich wet food importance, taurine requirements, and hydration fountains.
  • Rabbits: 80%+ diet consisting of fresh Timothy or Meadow hay with dark leafy greens.
  • Fish: High-grade micro-pellets and flakes balanced to prevent overfeeding and water pollution.

Please contact an emergency veterinary hospital immediately! While PetSathi provides preventative guidance and wellness education, online platforms cannot replace emergency clinical examination.

🚨 Red Flag Signs: Difficulty breathing, pale gums, inability to stand, sudden seizures, repeated vomiting, or acute abdominal bloating require urgent emergency hospital transport without delay.

As a standard veterinary recommendation:

  • Puppies & Kittens: Monthly checkups between 8 to 16 weeks for core immunization series and deworming.
  • Adult Pets (1–7 Years): At least one comprehensive annual physical wellness exam.
  • Senior Pets (7+ Years): Bi-annual (every 6 months) checkups with blood chemistry panels to catch arthritis or organ changes early.

Establishing the Nitrogen Cycle before adding any fish is the single most critical factor in fish health. Beneficial bacteria must colonize your biological filter media to convert toxic Ammonia into Nitrite, and then into less toxic Nitrate.

Check out our dedicated Aquarium Fish Care Guide for tank volume charts, water parameters, and compatible community fish species.

Mental stimulation is just as vital as physical exertion. Rotate toys weekly to maintain novelty, use lick mats or puzzle feeders for meals, practice 5-minute positive reinforcement trick sessions daily, and provide vertical climbing territories for indoor cats.

Pet Walking Services

Everything you need to know about finding walkers, safety protocols, and daily exercise routines.

Finding a walking companion on PetSathi is designed to be simple and transparent:

  1. Navigate to our Find a Walker section and choose your pet category (Dogs, Cats, Birds, or Rabbits).
  2. Review caregiver profiles, experience, neighborhood service radius, and past pet parent testimonials.
  3. Connect with your chosen walker to schedule an initial meet-and-greet session before finalizing walk schedules.

Safety and trust are at the core of PetSathi. Registered caregivers undergo identity verification, canine body-language assessment, and reference reviews. Furthermore, our community review system allows pet owners to leave genuine ratings after completed walks.

The safety of the animal always comes first. During high-heat weather (when asphalt temperatures can burn sensitive paw pads) or heavy monsoon downpours, walks can be rescheduled to cooler evening hours or substituted with indoor enrichment, gentle play, and relief breaks.

🐾 7-Second Rule: Place the back of your hand on the asphalt for 7 seconds. If it's too hot for your skin, it's too hot for your pet's paws!

Yes! Consistent routines are especially beneficial for dogs. You can partner with a designated regular companion for Monday–Friday morning and evening walks, or schedule flexible weekend adventure hikes.

Caregivers are encouraged to send photo updates, water break confirmations, and walk summaries so you have complete peace of mind while at work or traveling.

Pet Owners & Guidelines

Best practices for preparing your pet, multi-pet households, and building positive relationships.

We recommend a stress-free introduction following these steps:

  • Schedule an initial 10–15 minute calm meet-and-greet in a familiar neutral space while offering favorite treats.
  • Inspect your pet's walking gear (a secure Y-shaped harness and sturdy non-retractable 6-foot leash).
  • Inform the walker of any triggers (e.g. fear of bicycles, excitement around other dogs, or resource guarding).

Yes! Many walkers are comfortable handling two well-mannered bonded dogs from the same household. Simply specify your pet count and temperaments when reaching out to caregivers so they can confirm comfortable handling capacity.

A complete pet profile ensures the safest care. We recommend including:

  • Age, breed, weight, and vaccination history.
  • Veterinarian contact details and emergency clinic address.
  • Dietary allergies, medication instructions, and favorite reinforcement rewards.
  • Behavioral tendencies and leash manners.

Every pet has a unique temperament. PetSathi supports specialized care filters for solo-walk-only requirements, senior pets needing slow sniff walks, and caregivers trained in gentle positive-reinforcement techniques. Always disclose special needs upfront so we can pair you with the right match.

After completed walking or care sessions, owners can submit a star rating and written testimonial. Honest feedback helps top caregivers gain visibility and assists fellow pet parents in finding trustworthy companions.

Account & Platform

Registration, profile management, becoming a caregiver, and platform data privacy.

Getting started takes less than two minutes! Visit our Registration Page, enter your name, email, and choose a secure password. Once registered, you can personalize your pet profiles and connect with caregivers immediately.

If you love animals and want to offer walking or caregiving in your neighborhood, sign up as a caregiver during registration or submit an application through our Services Portal. We will reach out to guide you through verification and profile setup.

On the Login Page, click the “Forgot Password?” link. Enter the email address associated with your PetSathi account, and we will send you secure password reset instructions.

PetSathi adheres to ethical data privacy standards. Your sensitive contact information and residential address are never publicly exposed or sold to third-party advertisers. Only verified walkers with confirmed bookings can view essential walking pick-up details.

Yes! PetSathi is built from the ground up with a modern, mobile-responsive layout. You can access all care guides, messaging, and search features seamlessly from any smartphone, tablet, or desktop browser with zero app installation needed.
