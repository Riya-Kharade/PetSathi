01 / Overview & Principles

Introduction

Welcome to PetSathi ("we," "our," or "the Platform"). PetSathi is an open-source, community-driven platform created to empower pet parents, companion animal lovers, and independent pet caregivers with comprehensive pet care guides, walker search workflows, handover safety checklists, and community resources.

We believe that trust is the foundation of compassionate pet care. Protecting your personal information and being completely transparent about how your data is handled are core priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how it is used, how it is secured, and your rights concerning that data when you visit petsathi.org, explore our care portals, or utilize our interactive features.

Current Platform Status: PetSathi is actively developed as an open-source community platform under the Winter Edition of NSoC. In its current implementation, the frontend emphasizes client-side interactivity, local device storage, and demonstrable user interfaces. As PetSathi introduces centralized backend databases and production authentication services, this policy will evolve with clear, advance notices to our community.

Transparency Principle We only collect information necessary to deliver pet care coordination, safety protocols, and direct support. We never sell, monetize, or broker personal information to advertisers or third-party marketing companies.
02 / Data Collection Realities

Information We Collect

We believe in honest, accurate disclosure of the data actually touched by PetSathi. Based on our current codebase architecture, personal information is collected solely when you choose to interact with specific forms, tools, or support channels.

Account & Registration (Preview)

When testing our registration or login interfaces (register.html, login.html), users may submit a full name, email address, role selection (Pet Owner or Pet Walker), and password.

Demo UI • Client-side only

Contact & Inquiries

When submitting inquiries through our Contact Us page or FAQ inquiry form, we collect your full name, email address, message topic, and the text of your communication.

User Provided • Support communications

Pet Walk Safety & Handover Data

In our pet handover workflow (handover.html), owners and walkers input pet name, microchip/identification, behaviour notes, dietary/handling needs, emergency contact name, and phone number.

Operational • Safety & Emergency

Community Reviews & Feedback

When sharing experience feedback on our Reviews page, you may provide your display name, selected pet category, numerical star rating, and review comments.

Public Community Feedback

Local Browser Storage Data

PetSathi utilizes modern client-side browser storage (such as localStorage and sessionStorage) rather than tracking cookies to preserve your progress across page visits:

  • Care Routine Checklists: Interactive progress on bird and rabbit care pages (birds/care.html, rabbits/care.html) is stored directly in your browser's localStorage so you do not lose your completed steps upon reloading.
  • Pet Walk Handover Status: Step-by-step progress, pre-walk owner instructions, walker acknowledgements, and post-walk summaries are saved locally via localStorage under keys prefixed with pet_sathi_handover_.
  • Session Notifications: Read/unread filter state and sample notification alerts on the Notifications page are temporarily held in your browser's sessionStorage and clear automatically when you close your browser tab.

Technical & Automated Transmission Data

Like virtually all websites, when your browser requests pages, typography, or images from PetSathi and our trusted content delivery networks (CDNs), standard HTTP transmission headers are communicated. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the timestamp of the request. PetSathi does not run server-side visitor IP trackers or surveillance analytics.

03 / Purpose & Usage

How We Use Information

We use the information we handle strictly for legitimate, pet-centric purposes designed to facilitate care, safety, and community assistance:

  • Facilitating Pet Safety & Care Coordination: Pre-walk details (such as medical alerts, reactivity notes, and emergency phone numbers) ensure assigned walkers can safely manage pets in their care and contact trusted guardians immediately if an unexpected emergency arises.
  • Responding to Support Inquiries: When you reach out via our contact channels or support@petsathi.org, we use your contact details solely to research and reply to your questions, feedback, or technical issues.
  • Delivering Client-Side Convenience: Browser storage enables your care checklist completions, handover records, and notifications to persist seamlessly while you browse without requiring login friction.
  • Maintaining Platform Integrity & Accessibility: Aggregated, non-identifiable feedback helps our open-source contributors improve site accessibility (WCAG compliance), responsive layouts, and pet care resources.
What We NEVER Do PetSathi does not sell, rent, lease, monetize, or trade any personal information to data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party commercial marketing entities. We do not engage in behavioural cross-site tracking or automated profiling.
04 / Sharing & Disclosure Boundaries

Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information except in the very specific, operational scenarios outlined below:

Between Pet Owners & Walkers

Information entered into the pet safety handover checklist (such as pet identification, emergency phone contacts, and special dietary/handling needs) is made available between the pet owner and the assigned walker for the duration and safety of the pet care booking.

Infrastructure & CDN Providers

External service providers (such as Google Fonts, Cloudflare/cdnjs, and Unsplash) receive basic HTTP transmission data solely to deliver stylesheets, icons, and pet imagery requested by your web browser.

Emergency Pet Protection

In extreme, life-threatening veterinary emergencies where a pet companion is injured or missing during a walk, designated emergency contact information may be shared with licensed veterinary clinics or animal rescue personnel.

Legal & Regulatory Compliance

We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable statutory law, lawful court order, subpoena, or to protect the vital physical safety and legal rights of PetSathi, our users, or companion animals.

05 / Security Measures & Boundaries

Data Security

We take reasonable, industry-standard precautions to safeguard personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. In keeping with our transparency commitment, we describe only the actual security measures implemented on PetSathi today:

  • Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS): All web traffic between your browser and our hosting infrastructure (e.g. Netlify, GitHub Pages) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS and TLS protocols.
  • Same-Origin Browser Isolation: Data stored in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage is strictly bound by web browser same-origin policies, preventing external websites from inspecting your PetSathi checklists or handover records.
  • Client-Side Input Sanitization: Frontend forms employ strict HTML escaping, input trimming, and validation routines to protect against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and injection vulnerabilities.
  • Open-Source Code Transparency: All source code is publicly accessible on GitHub, allowing community peer review, vulnerability scanning, and timely dependency auditing.
Important Security Advisory for Users Because the current account registration and login interfaces operate as interactive client-side demonstrations, do not enter sensitive production passwords, financial account credentials, or payment card numbers into sample forms. Always protect your local device with strong lock screens and updated browsers.
06 / Browser Storage & Cookies

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Unlike many commercial websites, PetSathi's reviewed frontend does not set, read, or rely on first-party HTTP cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or advertising beacons.

Instead, we utilize client-side Web Storage technologies:

  • Local Storage (localStorage): Retains functional state such as care routine checklists and pet walk safety handover entries on your device until explicitly cleared.
  • Session Storage (sessionStorage): Temporarily holds in-memory notification preferences for the duration of your browser session.
  • Third-Party Resources: External asset providers (Google Fonts, cdnjs, Unsplash) handle inbound resource requests in accordance with their respective privacy and infrastructure practices. We do not use these resources as hidden analytics or surveillance trackers.

For a comprehensive, in-depth breakdown of our browser storage practices and instructions on how to inspect or restrict them, please visit our dedicated Cookie Policy.

07 / Your Controls & Choices

User Rights

You maintain full sovereignty over your personal data. Regardless of your physical location, PetSathi provides you with clear, direct controls to exercise your privacy rights:

Right to Access & Inspect

You have the right to know what information PetSathi handles. You can inspect all data stored locally on your device at any time using your browser's Developer Tools (under Application > Local Storage).

Right to Correction

You may freely revise, update, or overwrite checklist entries and handover details directly through the interactive forms on our platform, or request corrections to email correspondence.

Right to Deletion / Erasure

You can instantly and permanently erase all PetSathi data from your browser by clearing your browser cache and site data, or by clicking "Reset" on individual checklist pages.

Right to Opt-Out & Object

You may browse PetSathi without interacting with forms or local checklists. If you have sent us support emails, you may email support@petsathi.org to request deletion of your correspondence history.

How to Clear PetSathi Local Data in Your Browser

To permanently remove all saved care checklists, handover drafts, and notification states stored on your machine:

  • Google Chrome & Brave: Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Delete browsing data > Cookies and other site data, or press F12 > Application > Storage > Clear site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Go to Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data, search for PetSathi, and click Remove.
  • Microsoft Edge: Go to Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Clear browsing data > Cookies and other site data.
08 / External Providers & Integrations

Third-Party Services

To deliver accessible typography, icons, responsive pet imagery, and open collaboration, PetSathi incorporates carefully chosen external resources. Each provider operates under its own independent privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): Used to deliver the DM Sans and Manrope typefaces. Google Fonts requests are served without authentication cookies. Review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare: Used to deliver Font Awesome 6 icons and UI symbols via global edge caches. Review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash: Provides high-resolution pet photography for educational and informational purposes. Review Unsplash's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft): Hosts the open-source PetSathi codebase, issue trackers, pull requests, and discussions. When participating in discussions or opening issues, your activity is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Netlify: Provides static edge web hosting and continuous deployment for our live demonstration platform. Review Netlify's Privacy Policy.

PetSathi has no administrative control over external web properties linked from our site. We recommend reviewing the individual privacy notices of any external site you visit.
