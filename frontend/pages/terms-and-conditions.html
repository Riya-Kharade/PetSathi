5. Pet Owner Responsibilities

Chapter 05

Pet owners utilizing PetSathi to browse walking companions, explore care routines, or utilize walk handover checklists must adhere to stringent care and safety standards:

  • Full Health & Temperament Disclosure: Pet owners must disclose complete and truthful details regarding their pet's temperament, anxiety triggers, aggression tendencies, bite history, fear reactivity, prey drive, and compatibility with strangers or other animals.
  • Current Vaccinations & Preventive Care: Pets introduced to walking companions or caregivers must be up to date on core vaccinations (including Rabies, DHPP for dogs, FVRCP for cats), parasite control, and municipal licenses.
  • Safe, Functional Equipment: Owners must provide sturdy, escape-proof collars or harnesses, durable non-retractable leashes, secure identification tags, and clean waste bags. Faulty or frayed walking gear is strictly prohibited.
  • Emergency Veterinary Contacts: Owners must provide reachable emergency telephone contacts and the details of their pet's licensed primary veterinarian, agreeing that caregivers may seek emergency care if the owner is unreachable.
  • Financial Responsibility: Pet owners retain 100% legal, medical, and financial responsibility for any veterinary care, emergency interventions, or damage caused by their pet.

6. Pet Walker Responsibilities

Chapter 06

Independent pet walkers and caregivers listed in directories, exploring bookings, or utilizing PetSathi's handover tools agree to uphold the highest standards of professional and humane care:

  • Humane Treatment & Positive Handling: Walkers must treat all animals with patience, kindness, and gentle positive handling. Physical punishment, aversive tools, yelling, or rough handling is strictly prohibited.
  • Strict Leash & Containment Compliance: Pets must remain securely leashed and under continuous physical control at all times during outdoor walks. Pets must never be left unattended in public places or vehicles.
  • Adherence to Owner Instructions: Walkers must faithfully follow the owner's written care directions regarding walk routes, duration, feeding, hydration, and medication.
  • Accurate Handover Logging: Walkers must utilize walk checklists (such as PetSathi's Handover Checklist) responsibly, accurately recording walk completion, waste elimination, hydration, and any unusual symptoms observed.
  • Immediate Incident Reporting: In the event of an escape, injury, animal altercation, or sudden medical issue, the walker must immediately notify the pet owner and contact emergency veterinary services.
