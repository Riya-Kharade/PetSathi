Our Story & Purpose

Building a Trusted Digital Companion

Discover how PetSathi began as an open-source initiative dedicated to supporting pets, owners, and community walkers.

In today's fast-paced world, pet parents often face a difficult challenge: balancing long workdays, daily responsibilities, and unpredictable schedules with the critical need to ensure their pets get regular outdoor exercise, mental stimulation, and compassionate care.

PetSathi was founded as part of Nexus Spring of Code (NSoC) 2026 – Winter Edition to bridge this gap. The word "Sathi" means friend and companion. Our goal is to create an open, community-powered bridge between caring pet parents and dedicated walking companions right in their local neighborhoods.

More than just a walking service, PetSathi is a holistic knowledge and care platform—offering structured guidance on nutrition, exercise routines, behavioral cues, and wellness tips tailored to different species.

“We envision a world where no pet is left longing for a walk, and no owner feels overwhelmed by the responsibilities of daily care.”

Rooted in Compassion

Designed with unconditional love for all pets and the people who care for them.

The Challenge

Pet owners struggle with limited free time, irregular walking hours, lack of trusted local walkers, and scattered care advice across disparate websites.

Our Solution

A unified, open platform providing verified walker discovery, effortless booking requests, structured pet wellness profiles, and dedicated species care guides.

The Philosophy

Open-source, community-first, and transparent. We develop tools with zero corporate clutter—centering purely on animal happiness and community safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our platform, community, and services.

PetSathi is an open-source pet care and pet-walking initiative developed as part of Nexus Spring of Code (NSoC) 2026 – Winter Edition. It was created to bring pet owners, local walking companions, and animal care information together on a single welcoming platform.

Yes! Exploring pet care information, browsing species guides, learning about dog walking, and creating pet profiles are completely free. As an open-source initiative, PetSathi is built for community benefit.

PetSathi currently features dedicated content and guidance for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, and aquarium fish. As the platform evolves, additional pet categories and specialized care sections will be introduced.

You can sign up on our registration page, specify your pet experience, walking areas, and availability. Pet parents in your neighborhood will then be able to view your profile and send walking requests.

Because PetSathi is an open-source project hosted on GitHub, contributors can check the issue tracker, fork the repository, pick up frontend/backend tasks, improve design, and submit pull requests following our contribution guidelines.
