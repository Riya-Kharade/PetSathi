All content featured on PetSathi—including pet breed profiles, care checklists, nutrition guidelines, training articles, behavior recommendations, and caregiver directories—is published strictly for general educational and informational purposes.

  • Good-Faith Publishing: Content is compiled and maintained in good faith from public domain animal care literature, community experiences, and open-source contributions.

3. Veterinary Advice

Website content does not substitute professional veterinary care

PetSathi is not a veterinary hospital, clinic, or medical service provider. No team member, contributor, or administrator at PetSathi acts as your pet's licensed medical professional through this website.

Critical Veterinary Notice

Nothing contained on PetSathi should ever be interpreted, considered, or used as a substitute for professional veterinary medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, surgical assessment, or pharmacological prescription.

Always seek the direct counsel of a licensed, accredited veterinarian regarding any questions you have concerning a medical condition, dietary transition, medication, behavioral anomaly, or physical illness affecting your companion animal. Never disregard, delay, or modify professional veterinary advice on the basis of something you have read on this website.

4. Pet Health Information

Inherent limitations of health, dietary, and breed data

Articles detailing pet health, common symptoms, preventive wellness routines, or dietary recommendations (such as dog feeding amounts, cat hydration guides, or bird nutrition) represent generalized observations:

Diet & Nutrition

Feeding portions and ingredient suggestions are baseline estimates. Specific metabolic rates, food allergies, and pre-existing conditions require customized vet consultation.

Genetic Health Risks

Breed-specific health summaries (e.g. hip dysplasia, respiratory concerns) highlight known breed tendencies, not definitive diagnostic certainties for individual animals.

Medications & Remedies

PetSathi never prescribes or recommends pharmaceutical dosages. Never administer human or pet medications without express veterinary instruction.

Toxic Substances

While we list common toxic household items, plants, and foods, our lists are not exhaustive. When in doubt, consult poison control immediately.

5. Emergency Situations

Urgent medical scenarios demand immediate professional intervention

PetSathi does not provide emergency medical services or real-time triage.

In Case of Acute Pet Emergency

If your pet has suffered trauma, ingested a toxic substance, exhibits labored breathing, experiences sudden tremors, seizures, bloat, severe lethargy, unresponsiveness, or uncontrolled bleeding, do not search online or wait for community replies. Immediately transport your pet to the nearest 24/7 veterinary emergency hospital.

We encourage every pet parent to keep the telephone number, address, and operating hours of their local 24-hour emergency animal clinic and national pet poison hotline securely saved and readily accessible.

6. Pet Walking Services

Platform boundaries and user responsibilities for pet walking

PetSathi offers features designed to help pet owners connect with independent local pet walkers and browse caregiver profiles:

  • Independent Caregivers: Walkers, sitters, and caregivers listed on PetSathi are independent individuals or community members. They are not employees, agents, or contractors of PetSathi.
  • Verification & Due Diligence: PetSathi does not conduct criminal background checks, verify certification licenses, or supervise in-person walking sessions. Pet owners are solely responsible for conducting their own vetting, interviews, reference checks, and verifying that walkers have appropriate experience for their pet's temperament.
  • Safety & Handover Protocols: Both owners and caregivers are urged to utilize clear safety handovers, verify vaccination records, test secure collars/harnesses, and agree upon handling instructions prior to initiating walks.
  • Assumption of Risk: Handling companion animals involves inherent unpredictability (e.g. bites, escapes, collisions, leash reactivity). All walking engagements are undertaken at the mutual discretion and risk of the owner and caregiver.
