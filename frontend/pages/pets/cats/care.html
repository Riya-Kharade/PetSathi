Daily Foundations

Everyday Cat Care Essentials

Consistent daily habits, measured feeding, and clean hygiene form the basis of a happy feline life.

Daily Routine

Daily Cat Care

Establish consistent daily rhythms of measured meals, mental stimulation, and comfortable sleeping perches.

  • ✓ Scheduled morning and evening feeding routines
  • ✓ Daily fresh water refill and bowl washing
  • ✓ 15–20 minutes of active wand toy play

Nutrition

Feeding & Nutrition

Support your obligate carnivore with balanced protein, essential taurine, and high moisture wet meals.

  • ✓ High-protein formulation tailored to age
  • ✓ Daily wet food for optimal kidney hydration
  • ✓ Controlled calorie portions to prevent obesity
Explore Nutrition Guide ↗
Hygiene

Litter Box Care

Maintain spotless, stress-free litter hygiene to support urinary tract wellness and natural digging habits.

  • ✓ Scoop waste daily and refresh litter weekly
  • ✓ Maintain the N+1 box rule per household cat
  • ✓ Place boxes in quiet, accessible locations

Coat & Grooming

Grooming & Coat Care Standards

Gentle brushing, mindful bathing routines, and claw maintenance keep your cat comfortable and clean.

Coat Care

Grooming & Coat Care

Routine coat brushing removes loose undercoat, minimizes hairballs, and distributes natural protective skin oils.

  • ✓ Brush short-haired cats 2x weekly; long-haired daily
  • ✓ Reduces painful mats and hairball ingestion
  • ✓ Creates relaxing trust-building bonding moments
Grooming Techniques ↗
Hygiene

Bathing & Hygiene

Gentle waterless foam washes and cat-safe baths preserve delicate feline skin without causing undue stress.

  • ✓ Cats self-groom meticulously throughout the day
  • ✓ Use feline-safe, soap-free shampoo when bathing
  • ✓ Keep water lukewarm and dry thoroughly
Bathing Instructions ↗
Paw Care

Nail & Paw Care

Routine claw trimming and sisal scratching posts protect furniture while keeping feline paw pads healthy.

  • ✓ Trim claw tips every 2–3 weeks carefully
  • ✓ Provide sturdy vertical and horizontal scratchers
  • ✓ Inspect paw pads for dryness, cracks, or debris

Health & Wellness

Veterinary & Physical Wellness Checkups

Routine dental hygiene, sensory care, and preventative veterinary checkups ensure lifelong vitality.

Dental Health

Dental Care

Combat feline gingivitis and tartar accumulation through routine enzymatic brushing and dental checkups.

  • ✓ Brush teeth 3 times weekly with poultry-flavored paste
  • ✓ Veterinary dental exams detect resorptive lesions
  • ✓ Enzymatic chews and water additives aid breath
Dental Care Tips ↗
Sensory Health

Ear & Eye Care

Inspect delicate ears and eyes weekly for early detection of mites, excess discharge, or conjunctivitis.

  • ✓ Wipe eye corners with warm damp cotton pads
  • ✓ Check ears for dark coffee-ground mite wax
  • ✓ Seek vet care for squinting or cloudiness
Sensory Health Guide ↗
Preventative

Veterinary Checkups

Preventative wellness exams, rabies/FVRCP vaccines, and microchip registrations protect long-term vitality.

  • ✓ Annual wellness checkups for adult cats
  • ✓ Year-round flea, tick, and heartworm protection
  • ✓ Core vaccinations and baseline blood panels

Life Stage & Seasonal Care Guides

Tailored care guidelines for energetic kittens, dignified seniors, and seasonal environmental safety.

0–12 Months

Kitten Care

Nurture rapid growth, early socialization, litter habits, and immune development during the crucial first year.

  • ✓ High-calorie kitten food rich in brain DHA
  • ✓ 3-dose booster vaccine schedule at 8, 12, 16 wks
  • ✓ Gentle handling to build lifelong human trust

7+ Years

Senior Cat Care

Support golden-age felines with glucosamine joint care, lower-rimmed litter boxes, and warm resting nooks.

  • ✓ Bi-annual senior wellness exams and kidney panels
  • ✓ Elevated food bowls and orthopedic beds
  • ✓ Gentle assisted grooming for stiff joints

All Seasons

Seasonal Care

Adapt indoor comfort to summer heatwaves and winter drafts while removing seasonal toxic plants and holiday hazards.

  • ✓ Provide cool tile mats and hydration in summer
  • ✓ Eliminate toxic lilies, poinsettias, and cords
  • ✓ Draft-free warm sunny sleeping spaces in winter
