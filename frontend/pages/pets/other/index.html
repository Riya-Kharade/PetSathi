Pocket Mammal Husbandry

Small Pets: Hamsters, Guinea Pigs & Friends

Small pets bring immense joy to smaller living spaces, but they need specialized attention. Hamsters are solitary and strictly nocturnal, requiring a minimum of 450 to 600 square inches of unbroken floor space to prevent cage stress. Guinea pigs, on the other hand, are highly social herd animals that must always be kept in bonded pairs and require daily dietary Vitamin C supplements.

🌾 Unlimited Timothy Hay 🎡 Solid-Surface Exercise Wheel 📦 Aspen / Dust-Free Paper Bedding 🚫 Never Cedar / Pine Shavings

Aquatic Science & Art

Fish: Freshwater Aquarium Fundamentals

The secret to healthy fish lies not in cleaning the tank to sterile extremes, but in cultivating beneficial bacterial colonies through the Nitrogen Cycle. Prior to introducing any fish, allow your filter to establish over 3 to 6 weeks. Pair non-aggressive species with compatible water temperatures, perform 20% weekly water changes with dechlorinated water, and avoid overcrowding.

Ectothermic Care

Reptiles: Vivarium Lighting & Thermal Balance

Because reptiles cannot generate their own body heat, their terrarium must feature a distinct thermal gradient: a warm basking spot (90°F–105°F depending on species) and a cooler retreat (75°F–80°F). Full-spectrum UVB lighting is non-negotiable for diurnal reptiles to synthesize Vitamin D3 and metabolize calcium, preventing Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD).

☀️ T5 High Output UVB Light 🌡️ Digital Probe Thermometers 💧 Hygrometer Humidity Control 🦗 Gut-Loaded Live Feeders

Preventative Husbandry

General Pet Care: Universal Welfare Protocols

Whether your pet hops, slithers, or swims, sound preventative husbandry protects them from illnesses. Conduct weekly physical inspections (checking eyes, scales, fur, gills, and weight). Keep a dedicated exotic animal first-aid kit at home and locate an emergency exotic veterinarian before any medical crisis arises.
