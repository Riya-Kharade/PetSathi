Essential Rabbit Insights

Introduction to Pet Rabbits

Understanding your companion is the cornerstone of a fulfilling pet guardianship journey. Here is what makes rabbits unique and how to care for them responsibly.

🐇

Why Rabbits Make Wonderful Companions

Rabbits are quiet, clean, and deeply affectionate indoor pets that form strong emotional bonds with their human families and thrive on companionship.

  • ✓ Highly social creatures that thrive in gentle, calm household environments.
  • ✓ Quiet and apartment-friendly with subtle, expressive body language.
  • ✓ Naturally clean animals that can easily be litter-box trained indoors.
  • ✓ Form deep, loyal attachments once trust and mutual respect are established.
📋

Responsibilities of Rabbit Ownership

Caring for a pet rabbit is a rewarding 8 to 12 year commitment requiring dedicated indoor space, daily care routines, and watchful supervision.

  • ✓ Providing a spacious, predator-proof, and bunny-proofed indoor exercise area.
  • ✓ Ensuring unlimited fresh grass hay and clean drinking water are always available.
  • ✓ Protecting electrical wires, baseboards, and carpet edges from natural chewing.
  • ✓ Dedicating daily time for gentle interaction, playtime, and bonding sessions.
🩺

Importance of Regular Rabbit Care

Because rabbits are prey animals who instinctively conceal illness, consistent preventative healthcare and prompt attention are vital.

  • ✓ Establishing care with a specialized exotic veterinarian experienced with rabbits.
  • ✓ Timely spaying or neutering to prevent reproductive cancers and behavioural issues.
  • ✓ Vigilant monitoring of eating and droppings to prevent deadly GI stasis.
  • ✓ Regular nail clipping, dental checks, and gentle coat brushing during shedding.

Daily Care & Wellness

Essential Rabbit Care & Health Guidelines

Rabbits have delicate digestive systems, sensitive skin, and continuous tooth growth. Following these foundational care pillars ensures your bunny thrives in comfort.

🏠

Indoor Housing & Bunny-Proofing

Keep rabbits indoors in a roomy x-pen or bunny-safe room rather than cramped wire cages. Conceal all electric wires, protect wooden baseboards, and provide soft non-slip floor rugs to cushion their delicate hocks.

🧹

Hygiene, Litter & Grooming

Rabbits naturally choose a corner for toileting. Line a shallow litter pan with paper-based bedding and a generous heap of fresh hay. Brush their coat weekly (daily during heavy molts) and never bathe them in water.

🩺

Exotic Veterinary Checkups

Schedule annual wellness exams with an exotic vet who specializes in lagomorphs. Regular checkups inspect constantly growing incisors and molars, monitor weight, and provide preventative vaccination against RVHD.

Balanced Diet

Rabbit Food & Wholesome Nutrition

A rabbit's digestive tract requires constant high-fiber intake to maintain healthy gut motility and prevent life-threatening gastrointestinal stasis.

80-85%
Timothy Grass Hay

Unlimited fresh hay available 24/7 keeps teeth worn and digestion moving.

10-15%
Fresh Leafy Greens

Daily washed greens like romaine lettuce, cilantro, mint, and parsley.

5%
High-Fiber Pellets

Measured plain timothy-based pellets without seeds, nuts, or dried corn.

100%
Fresh Water Bowl

Heavy ceramic bowl cleaned daily encourages natural hydration.

🥬

Safe Daily Greens

Romaine lettuce, bok choy, cilantro, basil, mint, dill, celery leaves, and carrot tops. Rotate greens regularly to provide balanced micronutrients.

🍎

Healthy Occasional Treats

Small slivers of apple (seedless), blueberry, strawberry, or a thin coin of carrot. Offer treats in strict moderation to prevent obesity and upset stomachs.

🚫

Dangerous Foods to Avoid

Never feed chocolate, avocado, iceberg lettuce, onions, garlic, potatoes, bread, seeds, nuts, or sugary human snacks, which can cause severe digestive toxicity.

Play & Communication

Rabbit Behaviour & Safe Enrichment

Rabbits express joy, curiosity, and boundaries through distinct movements and sounds. Understanding these cues helps you build a deep, trusting bond with your companion.

🤸

The "Binky" & Zoomies

When a rabbit leaps into the air, twists their body, and runs energetic laps, they are doing a binky—the purest expression of bunny happiness and exuberance!

😴

The "Bunny Flop"

A rabbit rolling abruptly onto their side and lying completely flat indicates they feel 100% safe, calm, and deeply relaxed in their living environment.

🦷

Tooth Purring vs. Grinding

Soft, gentle clicking of teeth while being stroked is a rabbit's "purr" of contentment. Loud, grating teeth chattering signifies severe discomfort or pain.

🪵

Safe Enrichment & Chewing

Satisfy natural chewing and digging instincts with safe untreated applewood sticks, cardboard digging tunnels, willow balls, and woven seagrass mats.
