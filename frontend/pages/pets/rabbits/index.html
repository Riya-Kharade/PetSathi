Essential Bunny Insights

Quick Information for Rabbit Parents

Understanding lagomorph needs is the foundation of a rewarding bunny companionship. Here are fundamental guidelines to keep your rabbit healthy, secure, and thriving indoors.

Basic Housing & Bunny-Proofing

Rabbits require spacious, enriched indoor territory to run, binky, and stretch without cramped confinement.

  • ✓ Spacious indoor enclosures with at least 12 sq ft of pen space and daily room to exercise.
  • ✓ Cord protection by shielding all electric wiring in split-tubing or keeping behind baseboards.
  • ✓ Non-slip flooring with rugs or mats over slippery tiles to protect sensitive bunny hocks.
  • ✓ Indoor-only living to protect bunnies from extreme weather, temperature spikes, and predator stress.

Diet & Daily Fresh Timothy Hay

Lagomorph gut health relies on constant fiber digestion and chewing to maintain digestive motility and dental wear.

  • ✓ 80–85% fresh unlimited timothy, orchard, or meadow hay available 24/7.
  • ✓ Measured fresh leafy greens including romaine lettuce, cilantro, mint, and fresh parsley daily.
  • ✓ High-fiber plain pellets strictly limited to 1/8 to 1/4 cup daily per adult rabbit.
  • ✓ Fresh clean water supplied daily in heavy ceramic crocks for natural ergonomic drinking.

Hygiene & Gentle Grooming

Rabbits are meticulous self-groomers but need human assistance with seasonal shedding and claw maintenance.

  • ✓ Frequent gentle brushing to remove loose undercoat and prevent dangerous hairball blockages.
  • ✓ Strictly never submerge in water baths; water immersion induces shock and hypothermia.
  • ✓ Regular claw trims every 4–6 weeks using rabbit-safe clippers safely above the quick.
  • ✓ Litter tray hygiene with rabbit-safe paper pellet bedding and fresh hay topped daily.

Daily Care & Wellness Vigilance

As prey species, rabbits instinctively hide illness; daily vigilance and prompt veterinary action save lives.

  • ✓ Annual wellness checkups with an exotic animal veterinarian experienced in rabbit medicine.
  • ✓ Immediate emergency response for GI stasis if your rabbit stops eating or producing droppings.
  • ✓ Spaying and neutering to prevent fatal reproductive cancers and improve behavioral harmony.
  • ✓ Daily observation of bright curious eyes, clean ears, healthy appetite, and lively hops.
