Fur Care & Textures

Coat Types & Grooming Needs

Rabbits cannot vomit; grooming prevents wool ingestion and life-threatening GI stasis. Learn how coat textures dictate care routines.

Short-Haired

Flyback & Rollback

Standard sleek coats where guard hairs protect a dense undercoat. Fur naturally returns to place when stroked backwards.

Weekly brush (2–3x during molt)

Long-Haired

Wool & Mane Breeds

Features long strands around the crown (Lionheads) or full-body wool. Highly prone to debris accumulation and mats.

3–4 times weekly detangling

Rex-Coated

Velveteen Plush

Short, perpendicular guard hairs create an incomparable plush velvet feel. Sensitive skin requires gentle rubber grooming.

Gentle hand-wipes & soft brush

Angora Wool

High Maintenance

Abundant, fine specialty wool covering the entire bunny. Requires high dedication to prevent matting and wool block.

Daily combing + 90-day shears
Adoption & Compatibility

Choosing the Right Rabbit Breed

Consider how each breed's spatial, physical, and behavioral tendencies fit into your daily home routine before welcoming your companion.

Living Space

Giant breeds like Flemish Giants need large exercise pens or full rabbit-proofed rooms, while dwarfs still require 12+ sq. ft. of roaming space.

Ensure ample floor clearance

Grooming Needs

Assess your daily availability: Angoras and Lionheads demand consistent combing, whereas short-haired Dutch or Rex rabbits require simple weekly upkeep.

Prevent painful coat mats

Activity Levels

Energetic athletic breeds like English Spots thrive on tunnels and puzzle toys, whereas mature Flemish Giants often prefer relaxing lounging sessions.

Match household energy

Temperament

While handling comfort varies individually, Holland Lops and Dutch Rabbits are frequently celebrated for gentle curiosity and affectionate social habits.

Prioritize floor-level trust

Care Commitment

Domestic rabbits live 8 to 12+ years. Daily replenishment of unlimited fresh timothy hay, clean water, litter box upkeep, and exotic vet care are vital.

Long-term 10+ year journey
