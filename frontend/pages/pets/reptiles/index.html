Practical Terrarium Guide

Essential Information for Reptile Parents

Reptiles have unique physiological needs that differ significantly from mammalian pets. Follow these fundamental principles to ensure your terrarium companion stays thriving and healthy.

Terrarium & Habitat Setup

Enclosures must replicate your pet's native biotope—whether arid desert or humid tropical rainforest.

  • Dual Hiding Caves: Provide at least two secure, opaque hides—one in the warm basking zone and one on the cool side.
  • Substrate Safety: Use paper towels, slate tiles, or species-specific soil mixes; avoid cedar, pine, or loose fine silica sands.
  • Humidity Monitoring: Use reliable digital hygrometers to maintain exact species humidity; mist tropical setups daily.

Temperature & UVB Lighting

Reptiles are ectothermic and rely completely on external heat and ultraviolet rays for metabolic health.

  • Thermal Gradient: Maintain a warm basking spot (88°F–105°F) and a cooler retreat zone (72°F–80°F) for thermoregulation.
  • Thermostat Regulation: Always connect all heat mats, ceramic emitters, and radiant panels to digital pulse thermostats.
  • Linear UVB Output: Supply full-spectrum T5 or T8 linear UVB tubes; replace lamps every 6–12 months as output degrades.

Feeding, Nutrition & Supplements

A nutrient-dense diet combined with essential mineral dusting prevents painful nutritional deficiencies.

  • Calcium & D3 Dusting: Dust feeder insects or fresh greens with high-quality calcium powder and multivitamin supplements.
  • Gut-Loading Feeders: Feed live crickets, dubia roaches, and mealworms fresh squash and greens 24 hours prior to feeding.
  • Dechlorinated Water: Provide fresh, dechlorinated drinking water daily in shallow dishes that prevent accidental drowning.

Safe Handling & Household Safety

Gentle handling techniques and strict personal hygiene keep both reptiles and family members safe.

  • Support the Body: Scoop reptiles gently from beneath, supporting all four legs; never grab by the tail, neck, or limbs.
  • Hand Washing: Wash hands thoroughly with warm water and antimicrobial soap immediately before and after contact.
  • Sanitary Terrarium Care: Disinfect bowls and decorations using veterinary-approved F10 disinfectant away from kitchen areas.
