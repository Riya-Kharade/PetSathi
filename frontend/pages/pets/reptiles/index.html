Essential Reptile Insights

Quick Information for Reptile Parents

Understanding your companion is the cornerstone of a fulfilling pet parenthood journey. Here is what makes reptiles special and how to care for them responsibly.

Why Reptiles Make Wonderful Companions

Reptiles offer calm companionship, hypoallergenic presence, and fascinating natural behaviors.

  • ✓ Quiet, peaceful companions that do not bark or shed fur in the house.
  • ✓ Ideal for owners or children with allergies to dog or cat fur and dander.
  • ✓ Compact habitat footprint with beautiful naturalistic terrarium displays.
  • ✓ Fascinating natural behaviors, intriguing colors, and calm temperaments.

Responsibilities of Reptile Keepers

Responsible ownership means diligent maintenance of their artificial microclimate and diet.

  • ✓ Maintaining strict temperature gradients and daily UVB lighting schedules.
  • ✓ Supplying balanced live prey, fresh salads, and calcium with D3 supplements.
  • ✓ Routine vivarium spot cleaning and fresh, dechlorinated drinking water.
  • ✓ Thorough hand washing with soap before and after handling any reptile.

Importance of Proper Habitat & Care

Consistent environmental control and preventative care protect reptiles from common ailments.

  • ✓ Reliable digital thermostats prevent life-threatening overheating or chills.
  • ✓ High-quality UVB lighting enables proper calcium synthesis and prevents MBD.
  • ✓ Correct humidity levels prevent respiratory illness and ensure complete shedding.
  • ✓ Periodic checkups with an exotic veterinary specialist for long-term health.

Every Pet Deserves a Thriving Habitat.
