Popular Pet Bird Species

Every bird has different social, dietary, environmental and enrichment needs. Learn about these popular companion birds, their unique personalities and care requirements before deciding which species may suit your household.

10 Popular Species

Meet Our Feathered Companions

Explore the characteristics, approximate adult size, lifespan and care needs of different companion birds.

01
Small Parakeet

Budgerigar (Budgie)

Budgerigars are small Australian parakeets known for their colourful feathers, cheerful chirping and curious personalities. They can learn whistles and may mimic simple words.

Size About 18 cm
Lifespan 5–10 years
Care difficulty Moderate

Personality: Social, playful, active and curious.

02
Small Parrot

Cockatiel

Cockatiels are Australian parrots recognised by their expressive crests and orange cheek patches. They often enjoy whistling, gentle interaction and spending time with familiar people.

Size 30–33 cm
Lifespan 15–20 years
Care difficulty Moderate

Personality: Gentle, affectionate, expressive and social.

03
Small Parrot

Lovebird

Lovebirds are small African parrots known for their colourful plumage and strong social bonds. They are energetic birds that need regular interaction, toys and mental stimulation.

Size 13–17 cm
Lifespan 10–20 years
Care difficulty Intermediate

Personality: Energetic, bold, playful and affectionate.

04
Large Parrot

African Grey Parrot

African Grey Parrots are highly intelligent birds known for their vocal mimicry and problem-solving abilities. They require substantial mental stimulation, social interaction and long-term commitment.

Size 30–40 cm
Lifespan 40–60 years
Care difficulty Advanced

Personality: Intelligent, sensitive, observant and vocal.

05
Large Parrot

Macaw

Macaws are large, colourful parrots native to Central and South America. Their striking plumage and intelligence make them fascinating birds, but they need substantial space, enrichment and experienced care.

Size Up to 86 cm*
Lifespan 30–60 years
Care difficulty Advanced

Personality: Playful, intelligent, expressive and loud.

06
Large Parrot

Cockatoo

Cockatoos are recognisable by their expressive crests and powerful beaks. Many form strong bonds with caregivers and require extensive interaction, exercise and safe chewing activities.

Size 30–60 cm*
Lifespan 25–50 years
Care difficulty Advanced

Personality: Social, expressive, affectionate and demanding.

07
Songbird

Canary

Canaries are small songbirds well known for their beautiful singing and bright plumage. They are often enjoyed for their activity and varied songs, although many prefer gentle observation over handling.

Size 12–13 cm
Lifespan 10–15 years
Care difficulty Intermediate

Personality: Active, alert, independent and musical.

08
Small Songbird

Finch (Zebra Finch)

Finches are small, lively birds often kept in compatible groups. Zebra finches are popular for their cheerful calls and active social behaviour. They usually prefer observing rather than being handled.

Size 10–12 cm
Lifespan 5–10 years
Care difficulty Intermediate

Personality: Social, active, alert and gentle.

09
Small Parrot

Green-cheeked Conure

Green-cheeked conures are playful small parrots known for their curious nature. They enjoy climbing, exploring toys, learning simple activities and interacting with familiar caregivers.

Size 25–27 cm
Lifespan 15–25 years
Care difficulty Intermediate

Personality: Curious, energetic, playful and affectionate.

10
Parakeet

Indian Ringneck Parakeet

Indian Ringneck Parakeets are recognised by their long tails and curved beaks. They are intelligent, active birds known for their vocal abilities and distinctive appearance.

Size 38–42 cm*
Lifespan 25–30 years
Care difficulty Advanced

Personality: Independent, intelligent, active and vocal.

Responsible Bird Ownership Matters

Birds need much more than food and a cage. They require suitable living space, a balanced species-appropriate diet, safe enrichment, daily observation and access to an experienced avian veterinarian. Some parrots can live for several decades, so their care is a serious long-term commitment. Responsible adoption and legal, ethical sourcing are important.

Important for readers in India: The Indian Ringneck profile is included for educational identification, not as a recommendation to keep one. The Rose-ringed Parakeet is listed under Schedule II of India's Wildlife (Protection) Act. Never capture a wild bird. Check current requirements with the relevant Forest Department.
