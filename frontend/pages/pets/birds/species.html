LIFELONG STEWARDSHIP

Responsible Bird Ownership Matters

Birds require specialized knowledge, long-term commitment, and daily environmental enrichment. True care extends far beyond food and cage maintenance:

Spacious Flight & Safety

Provide the largest enclosure possible with varied natural perches, bird-proofed free-flight space, and freedom from toxic fumes (non-stick cookware, aerosol sprays).

Species-Tailored Nutrition

Supplement formulated pellets with vitamin-rich fresh vegetables, leafy chop, sprouted seeds, and clean drinking water daily to prevent deficiency disease.

Enrichment & Avian Care

Birds mask symptoms of illness instinctively. Schedule annual checkups with an experienced avian veterinarian and rotate stimulating foraging and puzzle toys.

Important for readers in India: The Indian Ringneck profile is included for educational identification, not as a recommendation to keep one. The Rose-ringed Parakeet is listed under Schedule II of India's Wildlife (Protection) Act. Never capture wild birds. Always adhere to official Forest Department guidelines and legal mandates.
