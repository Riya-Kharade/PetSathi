Canine Classification & Care

Dog Breed Information

Essential canine fundamentals across size tiers, family compatibility, daily activity, and coat maintenance.

Size Spectrum

Small, Medium & Large Breeds

Dog sizes range from compact toy breeds under 15 lbs to working guardians over 90 lbs, shaping living space, gear, and food needs.

Small (< 25 lbs): Pomeranian, Shih Tzu, Pug
Medium (25–55 lbs): Beagle, Indian Pariah Dog
Large (55–100+ lbs): Labrador, Golden, GSD, Rottweiler
Family & Kids

Family-Friendly Breeds

Celebrated for patient temperaments, reliable loyalty, and eager sociability with children and multi-pet households.

Key Traits: Patient, Tolerant, Affectionate
Best Matches: Golden Retriever, Labrador, Beagle
Social Need: High household companionship
Activity Balance

Active vs. Low-Energy

High-energy working breeds require 60–90+ mins of daily aerobic exercise, while calm lap companions prefer short scenic walks.

High Energy (60m+): Husky, German Shepherd, Labrador
Moderate/Low (30m): Pug, Shih Tzu, Pomeranian
Mind Games: Puzzle feeders, scent games, agility
Hygiene & Coat

Basic Grooming Requirements

Coat care varies from regular de-shedding raking on double coats to quick weekly brushing and daily facial wrinkle hygiene.

Double Coats: Husky, GSD, Golden, Pomeranian
Smooth Short Coats: Indian Pariah, Rottweiler, Beagle
Wrinkle Cleaning: Pug (daily facial fold wiping)

General Canine Characteristics & Lifespan

While genetics guide natural traits, early socialization and balanced routines define individual temperaments. Toy and small breeds often enjoy lifespans of 12–16 years, medium breeds reach 12–15 years, and large working breeds average 9–13 years.

Pack Mentality Craves family structure, positive praise, and daily togetherness.
Daily Exercise Regular walking stimulates canine senses and prevents behavioral anxiety.
Dental Hygiene Consistent teeth cleaning guards against plaque and systemic health risks.
Preventive Care Annual veterinarian examinations, core vaccinations, and parasite control.
