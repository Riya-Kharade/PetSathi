Canine Classification & Care

Dog Breed Information

Essential canine fundamentals across size tiers, family compatibility, daily activity, and coat maintenance.

Size Spectrum

Size & Stature

Canine sizes range from compact toy breeds under 15 lbs to athletic working guardians over 90 lbs, determining diet and living requirements.

Small (< 25 lbs): Pomeranian, Shih Tzu, Pug
Medium (25–55 lbs): Beagle, Indian Pariah Dog
Large (55–100+ lbs): Labrador, Golden, GSD
Family & Kids

Family-Friendly Breeds

Celebrated for gentle loyalty, patient sociability with children, and easy integration into active multi-pet family environments.

Key Traits: Patient, Tolerant, Affectionate
Top Matches: Golden Retriever, Labrador, Beagle
Social Need: High household companionship
Activity Balance

Activity & Energy Needs

Working breeds thrive on 60–90+ minutes of daily aerobic exercise, whereas companion breeds prefer moderate walks and indoor play.

High Energy (60m+): Husky, German Shepherd, Labrador
Moderate/Low (30m): Pug, Shih Tzu, Pomeranian
Mind Games: Puzzle feeders, scent exploration
Core Canine Pillars

General Canine Characteristics & Lifespan

Key physiological traits, behavioral patterns, and lifespan milestones that define canine wellness across all developmental stages.

Pack Mentality

Craves family structure, positive praise, and daily togetherness.

Daily Exercise

Regular walking stimulates canine senses and prevents behavioral anxiety.

Natural Lifespan

Canines average 10–16 years, with smaller breeds frequently outliving larger dogs.

Dental Hygiene

Consistent teeth cleaning guards against plaque and systemic health risks.

Preventive Care

Annual vet examinations, core vaccinations, and routine parasite control.

Mental Enrichment

Scent games and puzzle tasks maintain cognitive sharpness and emotional balance.
