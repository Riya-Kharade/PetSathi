Canine Classification & Traits

Dog Breed Information

Every breed carries unique physical characteristics, energy drives, and grooming commitments. Explore how size categories, family temperaments, exercise needs, and coat care shape each dog's daily life.

Size Spectrum
📐

Small, Medium & Large Breeds

Dog sizes range from compact toy breeds under 15 lbs to noble working guardians over 100 lbs. Size affects living space needs, daily feeding portions, joint health, and exercise equipment.

Small Breeds (< 25 lbs): Pomeranian, Shih Tzu, Pug
Medium Breeds (25–55 lbs): Beagle, Siberian Husky
Large Breeds (55–100+ lbs): Labrador, Golden, GSD, Rottweiler
Home & Kids
🏡

Family-Friendly Breeds

Renowned for their gentle patience, reliable loyalty, and eager sociability with children and multi-pet households. They form affectionate bonds and thrive on inclusive household activities.

Key Traits: Patient, Tolerant, Affectionate
Best Matches: Labrador, Golden Retriever, Beagle
Social Need: High interaction & indoor companionship
Activity Balance
⚡

Active vs. Low-Energy Breeds

High-energy working dogs need 60–90+ minutes of aerobic activity and brain games daily, whereas calm, low-energy companions are content with gentle scenic strolls and cozy indoor relaxation.

High-Energy (60–90m+): Husky, German Shepherd, Labrador
Low-Energy (20–30m): Pug, Shih Tzu, Pomeranian
Mental Stimulation: Puzzle feeders, scent games, agility
Hygiene & Coat Care
🧼

Basic Grooming Requirements

Coat care depends on fur architecture. Double coats need regular de-shedding raking without shaving; smooth coats require quick weekly brushing; and flat-faced breeds require daily wrinkle hygiene.

Double Coats: Husky, GSD, Golden, Pomeranian
Smooth Short Coats: Rottweiler, Dachshund, Beagle
Wrinkle Cleaning: Pug (daily fold wiping & drying)
Core Canine Foundations
🐾

General Canine Characteristics & Lifespan

While breed genetics shape natural instincts (such as tracking in Beagles or retrieving in Labradors), early puppy socialization, consistent positive reinforcement, and balanced daily routines have a profound impact on temperament. Toy and small breeds frequently enjoy lifespans of 12–16 years, while large and giant breeds average 9–13 years.

Pack Mentality: Craves structure, praise & family inclusion
Exercise Rhythm: Daily walks stimulate senses & reduce anxiety
Dental Care: Weekly brushing prevents periodontal disease
Vet Checkups: Annual wellness exams & core vaccinations
