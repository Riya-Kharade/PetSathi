Daily Care

Daily Dog Care

Build a simple routine that supports comfort, health, and good habits.

  • Daily care routineBalance walks, play, meals, and rest.
  • Cleaning & hygieneKeep bedding, bowls, and living areas clean.
  • Fresh waterRefresh clean drinking water throughout the day.
  • Comfortable sleeping spaceChoose a calm, draft-free resting spot.
  • Daily health observationNotice changes in appetite, movement, or mood.
Grooming

Bathing & Cleaning

Keep bath time gentle and suited to your dog's skin and coat.

  • Bathing frequencyAdjust baths to coat, lifestyle, and vet advice.
  • Choosing dog shampooUse a gentle product made for dogs.
  • Proper bathing stepsUse lukewarm water and avoid the eyes and ears.
  • Drying & cleaning tipsTowel dry well and keep your dog warm.
Grooming

Brushing & Coat Care

Regular brushing helps keep coats comfortable and free of tangles.

  • Types of dog coatsMatch the brush to your dog's coat type.
  • Brushing frequencyBrush more often when coats shed or tangle.
  • Removing loose furUse gentle strokes to lift loose hair.
  • Preventing tangles & matsWork through knots gently; never pull at skin.
Grooming

Dental Care

Good oral habits can help protect teeth and keep breath fresh.

  • Brushing your dog's teethIntroduce a soft brush gradually and gently.
  • Dog toothpasteUse canine toothpaste, never human toothpaste.
  • Dental chewsChoose an appropriate chew and supervise use.
  • Preventing bad breathAsk your vet about persistent odor or discomfort.
Grooming

Nail & Paw Care

Routine checks help keep paws comfortable on walks and at home.

  • Nail trimmingTrim gradually or ask a groomer for help.
  • Paw cleaningWipe paws after walks and check between toes.
  • Checking cuts & injuriesLook for swelling, debris, or tender spots.
  • Hot weather paw protectionAvoid hot pavement and choose cooler walk times.
Grooming

Ear & Eye Care

Gentle cleaning and early attention can help prevent discomfort.

  • Safe ear cleaningClean only the visible outer ear with vet-approved supplies.
  • Recognising ear infectionsWatch for odor, redness, head shaking, or scratching.
  • Eye cleaningWipe discharge gently with a clean, damp cloth.
  • Identifying irritationContact your vet about redness, squinting, or discharge.

Puppy Care

Puppy Care

Make the first weeks safe, calm, and full of positive routines.

  • New puppy essentialsPrepare food, bowls, a bed, and safe toys.
  • First-day home careLet your puppy explore at a relaxed pace.
  • Toilet training basicsUse a consistent schedule and reward progress.
  • SocialisationIntroduce new people and places gradually.
  • Puppy-proofing your homeSecure cords, chemicals, and small objects.
Senior Dog Care

Senior Dog Care

Small adjustments can make later years more comfortable.

  • Special care for older dogsAdjust activity and routines to changing needs.
  • Comfortable sleeping arrangementsOffer supportive bedding in an easy-to-reach spot.
  • Mobility supportKeep paths clear and use non-slip surfaces.
  • Regular veterinary checkupsDiscuss new changes and age-related needs early.
Safety

Dog Safety

Prepare your home and outings to reduce common risks.

  • Home safetySecure windows, bins, cords, and small objects.
  • Outdoor safetyUse identification, a secure lead, and close supervision.
  • Toxic household productsStore medicines, cleaners, and unsafe foods out of reach.
  • Emergency warning signsSeek urgent help for breathing trouble or collapse.
  • First-aid basicsKeep your vet's number handy; call before treating injuries.
Health

Vet Visits & Preventive Care

Work with your veterinarian to plan prevention and timely care.

  • Regular veterinary checkupsPlan routine visits based on age and health.
  • Vaccination scheduleFollow a schedule tailored by your veterinarian.
  • DewormingAsk your vet how often parasite checks are needed.
  • Tick & flea preventionChoose suitable prevention with veterinary guidance.
  • When to visit a veterinarianGet advice for persistent or sudden changes.
