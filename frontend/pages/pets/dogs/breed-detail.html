Behavior & Compatibility

Breed Characteristics & Lifestyle

Detailed assessment of temperament, exercise needs, family dynamics, and living suitability.

Personality & Temperament

Known for an eager-to-please nature, warm gentleness with children, and trustworthy sociability with guests and companions.

  • ✓ Outstanding gentleness and empathetic family bonding
  • ✓ Sociable and friendly with humans and other pets
  • ✓ Balanced confidence without unprovoked aggression

Exercise Requirements

Requires consistent daily outdoor workouts, brisk neighborhood walking, swimming, and mental stimulation games to prevent boredom.

  • ✓ 60 to 90 minutes of daily physical exercise
  • ✓ Loves fetch, retrieve games, and trail walks
  • ✓ Enjoys agility courses and puzzle feeder toys

Grooming Needs

Regular maintenance preserves skin health, prevents troublesome matting in feathering, and manages natural seasonal shedding.

  • ✓ Brush 2–3 times weekly using a slicker brush
  • ✓ Occasional bathing with gentle canine shampoo
  • ✓ Routine ear inspection and safe nail trims

Training & Behaviour

Exceptional working intelligence ensures rapid mastery of basic obedience, trick training, and specialized service duties.

  • ✓ Highly responsive to positive reinforcement methods
  • ✓ Early socialization builds steadfast adult confidence
  • ✓ Excels in agility, rally, and search-and-rescue tasks

Family Compatibility

Renowned as one of the world's most patient and affectionate family companions, flourishing in active households.

  • ✓ Exceptionally patient with toddlers and older kids
  • ✓ Loves participating in family weekend adventures
  • ✓ Harmonious coexistence with cats and fellow dogs

Apartment Suitability

Can adapt successfully to apartment or townhouse living provided their daily exercise and outdoor walking needs are faithfully met.

  • ✓ Moderate indoor activity when adequately walked
  • ✓ Requires dedicated outdoor park visits twice daily
  • ✓ Calm temperament indoors after exercise sessions
Care & Wellness

Health & Daily Care Routines

Actionable health guidelines, grooming practices, and nutritional requirements for long-term vitality.

Common Health Concerns

Awareness of hereditary vulnerabilities like hip dysplasia, skin allergies, and eye conditions enables proactive prevention.

  • ✓ Hip & elbow joint screenings from reputable breeders
  • ✓ Monitoring for ear infections in floppy-eared dogs
  • ✓ Routine eye examinations for cataract prevention

Grooming & Hygiene

A consistent hygiene routine safeguards against parasite infestations, skin irritations, and dental plaque build-up.

  • ✓ Weekly ear cleansing with veterinarian-approved wash
  • ✓ Tooth brushing 3 times weekly for healthy gums
  • ✓ Checking paw pads after hiking or rough terrain walks

Diet & Nutrition

Nutritionally complete meals calibrated to age, metabolism, and activity level maintain a healthy lean weight.

  • ✓ High-quality animal protein as the primary ingredient
  • ✓ Measured portion control to prevent canine obesity
  • ✓ Fresh water accessible 24/7 across all seasons

Daily Exercise

Routine outdoor stimulation regulates energy, prevents destructive behavior, and maintains strong cardiovascular health.

  • ✓ Morning and evening structured leash walks
  • ✓ Off-leash play in safe, enclosed dog parks
  • ✓ Mental scent work and interactive puzzle feeders

Regular Veterinary Care

Preventative wellness checkups catch emerging health conditions early, ensuring a long, thriving companionship.

  • ✓ Annual or bi-annual wellness exams and core vaccines
  • ✓ Year-round tick, flea, and heartworm prevention
  • ✓ Periodic blood panels for mature and senior dogs
