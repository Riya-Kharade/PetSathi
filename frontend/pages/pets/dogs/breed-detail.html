Breed History & Heritage

Golden Retrievers were originally bred in the Scottish Highlands during the reign of Queen Victoria by Dudley Marjoribanks, later known as Lord Tweedmouth.

Between 1840 and 1890, Tweedmouth sought an ultimate gundog capable of retrieving waterfowl seamlessly across both dense vegetation and cold highland lochs. The resulting breed combined superior scenting, gentle soft-mouth retrieval, and unfailing loyalty.

Interesting & Fun Facts

  • Known for their "soft mouth" instinct, gentle enough to carry a raw egg unbroken.
  • Ranked among the top 4 most intelligent dog breeds globally.
  • Frequently serve as service, therapy, guide, and search-and-rescue companions.
  • Have webbed toes and water-repellent undercoats that make them exceptional swimmers.
