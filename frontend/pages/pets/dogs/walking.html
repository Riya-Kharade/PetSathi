Daily Walking Needs

Most dogs require 30 to 90 minutes of daily physical exercise depending on breed, age, and energy level. Consistent walks prevent obesity and boredom.

🦮

Leash Manners & Training

Practice loose-leash walking using treats and positive reinforcement. Avoid forceful pulling and use ergonomic Y-shaped chest harnesses for safety.

👃

The Power of "Sniffaris"

Sniffing is mental fitness for dogs. Allowing your dog to pause and explore scents stimulates their brain and significantly reduces anxiety.

☀️

Weather & Paw Safety

In summer, test pavement heat with the 7-second palm test. Walk during cooler morning and evening hours, and always bring portable hydration.

🎾

Interactive Play & Agility

Complement walks with backyard fetch, agility tunnels, flirt poles, and tug games to exercise natural canine athleticism and build focus.
