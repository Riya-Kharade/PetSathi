01 • EVERY STEP COUNTS

Why Are Walks So Important?

Regular, appropriate exercise supports a dog's physical and mental well-being. Here are five reasons to make movement part of everyday life.

💪

Physical Fitness

Walking helps maintain muscle strength, mobility and overall physical conditioning.

🧠

Mental Stimulation

New scents, sounds and surroundings give dogs opportunities to explore and use their senses.

⚖️

Healthy Weight

Suitable activity supports healthy energy balance when combined with a balanced diet.

🐕

Socialisation

Positive, controlled exposure to people, places and suitable dogs can build confidence.

😊

Better Behaviour

Appropriate exercise and enrichment can help reduce frustration associated with unmet needs.

💞

Stronger Bond

Shared walks provide time for positive interaction and learning together.

02 • EVERY DOG IS DIFFERENT

Find the Right Walking Routine

A puppy does not exercise like a senior dog. Choose a profile to explore general walking guidance.

INTERACTIVE WALKING GUIDE

Who is your walking buddy? 🐾

Choose a life stage or energy level.

🐶

For your little puppy

Choose frequent, short, gentle outings and self-paced play. Growing joints need care, so avoid long forced walks and intense repetitive exercise.

Remember: There is no universal number of walking minutes for every dog. Exercise needs vary with breed, age, size, fitness, body condition and health. Ask your veterinarian for an individual plan.
03 • MOVE, PLAY & EXPLORE

Make Exercise Fun!

Mix walking with play, discovery and mental activities. Choose activities suited to your dog's age, fitness and health.

🏃

Running & Playing

Let fit, healthy dogs enjoy suitable movement and self-paced play in safe spaces.

🏠

Indoor Activities

Play gentle hide-and-seek or practise simple movement games indoors.

🧩

Interactive Toys

Food puzzles and suitable toys can provide mental stimulation between walks.

🎓

Training Games

Practise recall, simple cues and reward-based games to exercise the mind.

🌳

Outdoor Exploration

Allow supervised sniffing and exploration on appropriate walking routes.

Need a quick activity idea? 💡

🏠

Find the Treat

Hide a suitable treat in an easy-to-access spot and encourage your dog to sniff it out.

04 • SAFE PAWS, HAPPY WALKS

Walking Safety First

A safe walk starts before leaving home. Keep these essentials in mind whenever you take your dog outdoors.

✓

Use a Suitable Leash and Harness

Choose a properly fitted collar or harness and a secure leash appropriate for your dog's size.

✓

Carry Drinking Water

Bring fresh water and a portable bowl, especially on longer walks or in warm weather.

✓

Choose Safe Walking Routes

Avoid heavy traffic, broken glass, dangerous debris and unsafe areas.

✓

Keep Your Dog Supervised

Maintain control near roads, unfamiliar dogs, crowded places and public spaces.

✓

Clean Up After Your Pet

Carry waste bags and dispose of dog waste responsibly.

✓

Follow Local Pet Rules

Respect park rules, leash requirements, restricted areas and community guidelines.

🌡️
Important: Protect your dog from heat.

In hot or humid weather, choose cooler early-morning or evening hours. Check the pavement before walking: if it is too hot for your hand, it may be too hot for your dog's paws. Stop activity if your dog shows signs of distress and seek veterinary help when needed.
